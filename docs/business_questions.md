## Business Questions:

1. What % of orders are delivered late vs. the estimated delivery date, and is this trending up or down?
2. Does late delivery correlate with lower review scores, and by how much?
3. Do customers who receive a late/poor-review order have a lower repeat-purchase rate in the next 90/180 days than customers with on-time orders?
4. Which states/regions have the worst delivery performance, and is this a carrier or a seller-density issue?
5. Which product categories have the highest late-delivery rate?
6. Are certain sellers systemically responsible for delays (seller-level SLA breach rate)?
7. What is the actual cohort retention curve (month 0–6) for customers, and how does it split by "good first delivery" vs "bad first delivery"?
8. Is there a price-point effect — do high-value orders get delivered faster/slower than low-value orders?
9. What is the estimated revenue at risk if late-delivery-driven churn isn't fixed (using repeat-purchase gap × avg order value)?
10. Which payment types or installment plans correlate with order value and delivery experience?
11. How does freight cost as a % of order value vary by region, and does it explain some delivery delay patterns?
12. If you could fix delivery performance in only 3 states or 3 categories, which would move the retention needle most?
