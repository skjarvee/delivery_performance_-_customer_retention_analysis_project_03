## 03 - Date exploration: Key Findings

## Fact_o_orders

### Date boundaries for order purchase:
  - Our datasets starts from "2016-09-04"	and ends till "2018-11-12 00:00:00"
  - Range of the data: "2 years 2 mons 7 days"

## Delivery Days
  - Median delivery day to customer receive the order on 10 days from the day of purchase 
  - Median estimated delivery days are 23 days from the day of purchase
  - Most orders getting delivered earlier then the estimated delivery date.

## Dataset completeness:
  - As order purchase date as base i found that start of dataset some months were incomplete. 2016-09 to 2016-12 data is incomplete and 2018-09 to 2018-10 data is also incomplete.


## Flagged Forward:
  - Since start and end of the dataset is incomplete, decided to exclude them from analysis section. cause they have less order which will provide misleading insights.
  - Effective analysis window: 2017-01 to 2018-08. Cohort analysis additionally excludes final ~3 months of that window to preserve full 90-day observation for every cohort.
